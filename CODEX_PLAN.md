## 0. Context (read before touching anything)

Bible+ is a SwiftUI iOS 17 app (XcodeGen `project.yml`, SwiftData, RevenueCat,
TelemetryDeck, TikTok Business SDK) with an AI companion backed by OpenAI
through a Supabase edge function (`supabase/functions/chat`).

The branch makes three kinds of changes.

### A. Bible licensing (legal risk)
- **The app no longer calls any Bible API.** bolls.life, which served unlicensed NIV/ESV/NLT/NASB/MSG/NKJV, and bible.helloao.org were both removed.
- **All translations are bundled.**
  - KJV and WEB are in `Shared/Content/bible-kjv.json` and `bible-web.json`.
  - 17 more are in `Resources/Bible/bible-<ID>.json`, generated by `scripts/bundle_bibles.py`.
- **`Shared/Services/TranslationLicensePolicy.swift` is the single allowlist.** It contains only public-domain texts and CC BY / CC BY-SA texts that allow commercial use and derivatives.
- **`BibleTranslation` keeps its `.niv/.esv/...` cases only so old saved data still decodes.** Always read the value through `.licensed`, and offer only `BibleTranslation.available` in pickers.
- **`BiblePlus/Services/LicenseCompliance.swift` runs on launch.** It migrates users off removed translations and deletes old downloaded Bible caches.

### B. App Store fixes
- The review prompt was removed from onboarding, along with the custom "Enjoying Bible+?" pre-prompt (`ReviewService.swift`).
- **AI consent:**
  - `AIConsent.swift` and `AIConsentView.swift` show a consent screen before any data goes to OpenAI.
  - Every AI network call in `AIService.swift` throws `AIError.consentRequired` until the user consents.
  - Consent can be revoked in Settings › AI & Privacy (`MemorySettingsView.swift`).
- **AI safety:** crisis rules live in the `AIService` prompt and in the server policy (`supabase/functions/chat/index.ts`). There is a disclaimer line under the chat input and a "Report Response" item in the bubble context menu.
- **Tracking:** TikTok is initialized only after the user grants ATT (`TikTokAnalyticsService.swift`).
- **Privacy manifests** were updated in `Resources/PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy` and `BiblePlusWidgets/PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy`.
- **Paywall** (`PaywallContainerView.swift`, `StoreKitService.swift`):
  - Restore shows a result.
  - Trial copy appears only for eligible users.
  - Auto-renew disclosure added.
  - "Continue with the free version" added.
  - False claims removed.
- **Info.plist:** added `NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription`; corrected the mic and speech strings.

### C. Website
`website/privacy.html`, `terms.html` and `support.html` were rewritten to be accurate.

### Do NOT undo
- Never re-add NIV, ESV, NLT, NASB, NKJV, MSG, CSB or any copyrighted translation. Never add any Bible API.
- Never send data to OpenAI before `AIConsent.isGranted`.
- Never add a custom review pre-prompt, or any review prompt in onboarding.
- Never start TikTok before ATT authorization.
- Don't rotate the Supabase anon key until the new build is live in the App Store (see Task 6).

## Task 2: Verify behaviour in the simulator

Delete the app first so you get a fresh install, then check each item:

   - The flow goes from the commitment screen straight to the paywall, with **no review prompt**.
   - There's no "92% of members" stat. The proof screen shows Psalm 1:2.

   - The renewal disclosure is visible.
   - The links read "Terms of Use (EULA)" and "Privacy Policy".

3. **Chat:**
   - The first send shows the consent sheet. "Not Now" keeps the typed text and sends nothing.
   - "Allow and Continue" sends the message.
   - Settings › AI & Privacy has a toggle; turning it off makes the next send show the sheet again.
   - The voice button also gates on consent.
   - The disclaimer line shows under the input.
   - Long-press on an AI reply shows "Report Response", which opens a pre-filled email.

   - The translation picker shows KJV, WEB, BSB, ASV, YLT and DRA, and each one loads Genesis 1 and John 3.
   - With BSB active, the toolbar badge shows "BSB".
   - KJV shows the UK Crown notice under the chapter.
6. **Other languages:**
   - Switch the app language to Spanish: the reader loads Reina-Valera 1909 with Spanish book names, and has no network traffic to any Bible API.
   - Switch to Indonesian and open Psalms. TSI doesn't include Psalms, so the reader should fall back to KJV, show the banner "This book isn't included…", and show the badge "KJV".

10. **Voice picker:** shows the "AI-generated" note.
11. **ATT:** declining tracking means no TikTok SDK init (add a breakpoint in `TikTokAnalyticsService.initializeSDK`).
12. **Save Image** from a verse-image share sheet works and asks for Photos permission without crashing.

## Task 3: Translate the new user-facing strings

The catalog is `Resources/Localizable.xcstrings` (source language `en`, about 550 keys). It covers the 40 locales in `project.yml` `knownRegions`, which match `Shared/Models/SupportedLanguage.swift`.

1. Build once in Xcode so the new `Text("…")` and `String(localized:)` keys are extracted into the catalog. Or run `python3 scripts/extract_strings.py --merge`.
2. Find the keys with no translations. The new strings come from these files:
   - `AIConsentView.swift`
   - `PaywallContainerView.swift`: CTA subtitles, `renewalDisclosure`, "Continue with the free version", "Restore Purchases", "Terms of Use (EULA)", "Privacy Policy".
   - `ChatView.swift`: the disclaimer.
   - `ChatBubbleView.swift`: "Report Response".
   - `MemorySettingsView.swift`: the "AI & Privacy" section.
   - `SettingsView.swift`: "AI & Privacy" and the restore alert.
   - `VoicePickerView.swift`: the AI-voice note.
   - `ChapterReaderView.swift`: the fallback banner.
   - `BibleTranslationPickerView.swift`: subtitles.
   - `TranslationLicensePolicy.swift` and `BibleReaderViewModel.swift`: attribution and Crown notice.
   - `ConversationalOnboardingView.swift`: "This shapes the verses and prayers we choose for you."
   - `OnboardingProofStatsView.swift`: "A few minutes a day, every day."
3. Translate them into all 40 locales, either with `OPENAI_API_KEY=… python3 scripts/translate_catalog.py` or by hand.
   - Keep `%@` / `%lld` placeholders.
   - Keep "Bible+", "OpenAI", "KJV", "988", "911" and "112" unchanged.
   - Keep the meaning legally exact for the consent and renewal text. Don't soften "explicit permission" or "auto-renews".
4. **Do not translate Scripture.** The Psalm 1:2 line in `OnboardingProofStatsView` is KJV English text. Either leave it in English everywhere, or swap in the localized verse from that language's bundled translation in `Resources/Bible`. Never machine-translate a verse and present it as Scripture.
