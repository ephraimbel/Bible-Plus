Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Bible Plus ("we," "our," or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, who we share it with, and the choices you have. Bible Plus does not require an account; most of your data never leaves your device.

1. Information We Collect

Profile information you provide: During onboarding you may share your first name, faith level, life seasons, the things you are carrying (for example grief, anxiety, or financial stress), prayer times, and Bible translation. Some of these answers concern your religious beliefs and wellbeing, which may be sensitive. This information is stored on your device.

AI companion data: If you use the AI companion, your messages (including voice messages converted to text), your first name, your onboarding answers, and short "memory" notes the app keeps about past conversations are sent to generate responses (see Section 3). Chat history is stored on your device. Because AI requests include your name and message content, this data may be linked to you.

Journal: Journal entries are stored only on your device and are never sent to the AI or to us.

Usage analytics: We collect anonymized product-interaction events (for example "plan started" or "paywall viewed") through TelemetryDeck to understand how the app is used. These events are not linked to your name or identity.

Purchase information: Payments are processed by Apple. We receive subscription status and purchase history (not your payment details) through RevenueCat.

Advertising attribution (only with your permission): If you allow tracking when iOS asks, the TikTok Business SDK receives your device's advertising identifier, an app-generated user identifier, and events such as onboarding completion, trial start, and purchase, so we can measure our ads. If you decline, the TikTok SDK is never started and nothing is sent to TikTok.

Support requests: If you email developer support or report an AI response, your email address and the content you choose to send are used to respond to that request. Reports open in your email app so you can review the message before sending. Our shared developer support inbox is hello@nursemind.app; include Bible+ in the subject.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Personalization: To tailor daily verses, prayers, plans, and devotionals.
  • AI responses: To generate the AI companion's replies.
  • Audio: Bible chapter text (no personal data) is converted to speech.
  • Improving the app: Anonymized analytics help us fix bugs and improve features.
  • Subscriptions and advertising measurement as described above.

3. Third-Party Services

  • OpenAI (AI companion and voices): Your AI companion data described in Section 1 is sent through our secure server to OpenAI's API to generate responses and AI-generated audio. We ask for your explicit permission before sending any personal data to OpenAI, and you can withdraw it anytime in Settings › AI & Privacy. Under OpenAI's API terms, data sent through the API is not used to train OpenAI's models and may be retained by OpenAI for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring. See OpenAI's Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase: Hosts the secure server that relays AI requests to OpenAI. We do not store your chat messages on it. It caches generated Bible audio, which contains no personal data.
  • RevenueCat: Manages subscriptions using an anonymous app user ID. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.
  • TelemetryDeck: Privacy-focused, anonymized analytics. See TelemetryDeck's Privacy Policy.
  • TikTok (only if you allow tracking): Advertising attribution as described in Section 1. See TikTok's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple: App Store purchases and on-device or Apple-server speech recognition for voice chat. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Storage

All Bible translations are built into the app, so reading and searching Scripture sends no requests to any outside Bible service. Your profile, saved verses, highlights, notes, journal, chat history, and AI memory are stored locally on your device. We do not maintain user accounts or a cloud database of your personal information.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the service providers listed above, only for the purposes described, and with TikTok only if you allow tracking.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Clear the AI's memory anytime in Settings › AI & Privacy, and delete individual conversations from the chat list.
  • Turn off "Share data with AI provider" in Settings › AI & Privacy to stop all data going to OpenAI.
  • Deleting the app removes all data stored on your device.
  • We do not retain your chat messages on our servers after a response is delivered.
  • Change tracking permission anytime in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

7. Children's Privacy

Bible Plus is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The AI companion is intended for users 13 and older; users under 18 may use it only with a parent's or guardian's permission, which the app asks users to confirm before first use.

8. Tracking

Bible Plus asks your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before any tracking. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track," the TikTok SDK is not started. Anonymized analytics (TelemetryDeck) are not used for tracking or advertising.

9. Notifications

If you opt in, the app schedules local notifications on your device based on your selected times. No data is sent to an external push notification service. You can disable notifications anytime in iOS Settings.

10. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal data and to withdraw consent. Because most data is stored only on your device, you can exercise many of these rights directly in the app. For anything else, email hello@nursemind.app.

11. Auto-Renewable Subscriptions

Bible Plus Pro is offered as auto-renewable Monthly and Yearly subscriptions through the Apple App Store; the Yearly plan may include a free trial for eligible new subscribers. Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase (or when the free trial ends). Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the period ends. Manage or cancel in your Apple ID subscription settings.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time and will post the updated version here with a new "Last updated" date. Material changes will be highlighted in the app.
